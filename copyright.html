Sources and removal requests

Prepline is a practice site for students preparing for the NUET. Many of its tests are built from past papers and mock tests that are already shared publicly for exam preparation. We name the source on every test page and remove material at the request of its rights holder.

Where the questions come from

Other NUET mock testsPractice compilations, trial tests and mock exams shared publicly by students and teachers. Rights belong to their authors. Prepline originals© Prepline. If you own material used here

  • Send the form below: who you are, which tests or questions, and what you would like us to do.
  • We remove the material within 2 working days of a request and confirm by e-mail. Nothing needs to be proven first.
  • If you would rather keep it on the site with a credit or a link to your own course, tell us that instead.
