How points work

  1. 1 point for every question you get right in a test or in Train by topic. Each question counts once a month, so retaking a test adds only the questions you missed before.
  2. 1 point for every correct answer in a live room or a duel.
  3. +5 points for winning a duel (more correct answers than your opponent).
  4. Normal practice tests do not count — only games played together, where everyone starts at once.
  5. Ties are broken by accuracy: share of answered questions you got right.
