Can I get Premium for free?

Yes. Invite friends with your link from the dashboard: every friend who signs up and finishes one test gives you a week of Premium.

How do I pay?

With any bank card on a secure page of Lemon Squeezy, a payment company owned by Stripe. The receipt comes to your e-mail. Prepline never sees your card details.

When does Premium switch on?

Usually within a minute after the payment. Come back to this page or any test and refresh it. Use the same e-mail as your Prepline account.

How do I cancel?

Open the link in your receipt e-mail and press Cancel. Premium stays on until the end of the month you paid for, and nothing more is charged.
